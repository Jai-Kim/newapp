# Privacy & how AI is used in this app

*Draft for review — not yet legally cleared. See `README.md` in this folder
for the open questions this text still needs answered before launch.*

This is a plain-language explanation of what happens to what you type, and
what your child reads, in this app. It is not a substitute for a lawyer's
review, but everything below describes what the app actually does — nothing
here is aspirational.

## Your stories are written and illustrated by AI

When you tell us what tonight's story should be about — a lesson, a
situation your child is going through, a fear, a change at home — that text,
together with the story details we already have about your child (their
first name, age band, language, interests, and earlier chapters), is sent to
**Anthropic** (the Claude models) to write the chapter, and to **Google**
(the Gemini models) to illustrate it.

We use these companies because generating an original, dual-language
children's story with consistent illustrations is not something we do
ourselves — it takes a large language model and an image model. Under our
commercial agreements with them, neither company uses your family's data to
train their models. `TODO(Jai): confirm this against the actual, executed

## Every chapter is checked before your child sees it

Generated text and images pass through an automated content filter before a
parent ever sees them. Then a parent — you — reviews and approves or rejects
every single chapter before it becomes readable. Nothing generated by AI is
ever shown to your child without that human step in between.

## Stories and illustrations are AI-generated

Every chapter and every illustration in this app is created by AI models,
reviewed by a parent, not drawn or written by a person. You will see a
"Made with AI" label on chapters in the app so this is never ambiguous.

## What we store, and where

Your account, your child's profile (first name, age band, language,
interests, and a locked character reference used so illustrations stay
consistent), and every chapter (text and images) are stored in our database,

## What consenting means

Before you can generate your child's first chapter, we ask you to read and
agree to this notice. We record that you agreed, and which version of this
notice you agreed to, against your account — so if we ever change what this
notice says in a way that matters, we can ask you to agree again rather than
quietly relying on an old agreement.
