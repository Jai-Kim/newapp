# Chapter 2 — The Wick That Drinks Slowly
## 천천히 물을 마시는 심지

*Lesson: some things take time, and waiting is its own kind of patience*

**English summary (canonical, drives retrieval):**
> On a rainy evening after supper, Mia tries again to make the old brass lantern light, and Grandma shows her that the dry wick must slowly drink its oil before any flame can come. While they wait, Grandma reveals that the lantern was made for her when she was a small, frightened girl, and Nubi the owl returns to wait at the window beside Mia until bedtime.
