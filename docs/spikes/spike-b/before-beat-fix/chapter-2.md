# Chapter 2 — Mia and the Slow Little Light
## 미아와 천천히 오는 작은 빛

*Lesson: some things take time, and waiting is its own kind of patience*

**English summary (canonical, drives retrieval):**
> On a quiet rainy evening after supper, Mia tries again and again to light the old brass lantern, and again it stays dark. Mom shows her a jar of green plums and sugar that will not become syrup until summer, and Sori — who returns to the window exactly as promised — explains that the lantern doesn't drink matches, it drinks slow things like moonlight and rain-quiet, one drop at a time. By bedtime Mia sees a glow no bigger than a pinprick, and instead of hurrying it, she lets it grow while she sleeps.
