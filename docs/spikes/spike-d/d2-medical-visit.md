# Chapter 4 — The Lantern Goes Outside
## 등불이 바깥으로 나간 밤

*Lesson: being brave at the doctor, and that it is okay to cry*

**English summary (canonical, drives retrieval):**
> The night before her vaccination, Mia is silent with fear until Grandma invites her to carry the newly lit brass lantern outdoors for the first time. Following Nubi through the drizzle, they find the owl's real home in a hollow of the old plum tree, and Mia finally says out loud that she is frightened. At the clinic the next morning she cries — and goes anyway, learning that tears and bravery can share one small heart.
